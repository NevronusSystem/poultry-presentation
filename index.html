5. Challenges in Remote Places

  • Professional Shortage: Acute lack of qualified doctors in rural areas.
  • Geographical Barriers: Difficult to transport large flocks to sub-district hospitals.
  • Reliance on Informal Aid: Farmers often depend on untrained "para-vets" or "quacks".
  • High Costs: Private veterinary visits are prohibitively expensive for smallholders.
